# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

- Quest search: Questie's quests found by name, each row saying where you stand with it (Ready to turn in, In your log, Needs level N, Available), its level, zone and giver, with the game's quest mark for its state.
- Directions: a click sends EasyFind's GPS to the giver, the first open objective, or the turn-in, by the quest's state.
- Tooltip: the quest's objectives, live progress while it is in your log.
- Scope: `@quest` lists only quests, your log first.
- Filters: Show (Not turned in, Available to me, In my log, Turned in, All) and Near me.
- Clients: Classic Era, TBC Anniversary, Mists Classic and WoW Forever.

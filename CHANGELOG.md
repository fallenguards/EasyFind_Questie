# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Quest search: Questie's quests found by name in EasyFind, each row with its level, zone and giver.
- Directions: a click sends EasyFind's GPS to the giver, the first open objective, or the turn-in, by the quest's state.
- Tooltip: the quest's objectives, live progress while it is in your log.
- Scope: `@quest` lists only quests.
- Filter toggles: Available to me, In my log, Hide completed, Near me.
